# 23. User Training Guide (Role-Based Workflows)

| Role | Primary Screens | Daily Operational Workflow |
|---|---|---|
| **Executive Chef** | ME Studio, Menu Items, Menu Planning, Portion Planning, SILA Store | Compose cyclic/buffet/event menus in ME Studio, link approved recipes, verify kitchen capacity & allergen integrity, submit for approval. |
| **F&B Manager** | Menu Performance, Demand Planning, What-If Simulation, ME Studio | Review Stars/Plowhorses/Puzzles/Dogs matrix, test guest-count/price scenarios in What-If & Twin Lab, approve & publish menus. |
| **Cost Controller** | Cost & Margin Analysis, Consumption Analysis, POS Mapping | Reconcile theoretical vs. actual consumption, audit unexplained variance & waste, review ambiguous POS semantic mappings. |
| **Finance Controller** | Cost & Margin Analysis, AI Menu Intelligence | Audit Food Cost %, Contribution Margin (distinct from Net Profit), and realized vs. estimated AI savings before period close. |
| **Procurement Manager** | Purchase Recommendations, AI Menu Intelligence | Review net ingredient shortages after inventory/open POs/shelf life, convert approved recommendations to procurement PO workflows. |
| **Storekeeper** | SILA Store (`?next=/mobile`), SILA Voice, Consumption Analysis | Check daily ingredient readiness, log waste/returns, execute stock counts & transfers, act on near-expiry alerts. |
| **General Manager** | ME Studio, AI Menu Intelligence, Menu Performance | Monitor property-level food cost target adherence, approve high-materiality autonomous action plans, inspect executive summaries. |
| **System Administrator** | Setup (`/menu-engineering/setup`) | Run 20-step onboarding wizard, manage tenant/property feature flags, configure AI budgets, autonomy policies, and staged data imports. |
